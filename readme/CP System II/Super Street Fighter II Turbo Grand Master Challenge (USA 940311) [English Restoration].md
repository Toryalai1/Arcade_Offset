Super Street Fighter II Turbo Grand Master Challenge USA 940311 English Restoration by strygo:

        [Details]

            Super Turbo came in two versions. Japan got Super Street Fighter II X:
            Grand Master Challenge, the one competitive players swear by. The rest
            of the world got a slightly different game: it plays differently, the
            continue screen is bare, most win quotes are gone, the endings are
            shorter, and Akuma's story is missing. Japan's version was in Japanese.

            This is Japan's game in English, under a US identity. The fighting is
            untouched. Everything you read is new, translated from the Japanese
            with Capcom's own English as the style guide.

        [Changes]

            - Japan's game, exactly as it plays in Japan
            - Super Street Fighter II Turbo: Grand Master Challenge title, US region
            - Balrog, Vega, M. Bison and Akuma: the Western names
            - All 136 win quotes in English
            - The continue screen's strategy tips in English
            - Every ending in full, in English, including Akuma's
            - Akuma's intro dialogue, cut from the export game
            - Free Play available

        [Setup]

            - Requires the stock, unmodified Japanese 940311 set: ssf2xj.zip, or a
              merged ssf2t.zip, in games/mame (split sets also need qsound.zip).
              Nothing on the card is modified; the restoration is applied in
              memory as the game loads. Only the program and graphics ROMs change,
              at their original sizes, with the original key.
            - Runs on Jotego's jtcps2 core.
            - Saves and settings use their own name, ssf2tgmc, so the stock game's
              settings are untouched.
