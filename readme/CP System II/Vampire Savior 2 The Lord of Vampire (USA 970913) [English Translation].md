Vampire Savior 2 The Lord of Vampire USA 970913 English Translation by strygo:

        [Details]

            Vampire Savior 2 is a Japan-only update of Vampire Savior with a
            shuffled roster: Donovan, Huitzil and Pyron join, while Gallon, Aulbath
            and Sasquatch sit out, and it carries its own endings. It never came out
            in English. This patch translates the entire game.

            Where the dialogue matches the Western release of Vampire Savior, the
            patch reuses Capcom's own English. The lines unique to this version,
            including every ending and the final confrontation with Jedah, are
            freshly translated. The title keeps the Vampire Savior 2 name, as the
            game appeared in arcades; "Darkstalkers 3" was a later home-version name.

        [Changes]

            - USA region presentation
            - Western character names on character select, route map and fight HUD:
              Bulleta -> Baby Bonnie Hood, Zabel -> L.Raptor, Lei-Lei -> Hsien-Ko,
              Phobos -> Huitzil
            - All 241 post-fight quotes in English
            - All 29 ending pages and both Jedah final-boss dialogues translated
            - Route stage names and option labels match the official U.S. wording

        [Setup]

            - Requires the stock, unmodified vsav2.zip in games/mame
              (split sets also need qsound.zip). Nothing on the card is modified;
              the translation is applied in memory as the game loads.
            - Runs on Jotego's jtcps2 core.
            - Saves and settings use their own name, vsav2en.
